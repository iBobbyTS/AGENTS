# Reviewer orchestration

## Two independent coverages, not a clean streak

Every PLAN and accepted business parent needs **two valid CLEAN records**, Clean A and Clean B, covering the final candidate; once a slot is retired as silent below, its carried CLEAN plus the productive slot's final-boundary clean close the pair. These are logical coverage slots. They need not be consecutive physical calls. The embedded [dispatch profile](model-routing.md) supplies each route; an explicitly selected same-provider policy still requires fresh independent contexts and records both as native. It must never be relabeled cross-provider. Main alone owns admission, actual identity and acceptance.

Run A's bounded initial discovery and material correction, then B's independent final-boundary pass. Do not feed A's desired answers or rejected hypotheses to B. A reviewer never accepts its own implementation or delegates its job. PLAN reviewers do not implement the plan. A valid clean can include NIT_DEBT / PLAN_NIT / SCOPE_PROPOSAL: these do not block or invalidate evidence merely because the report contains findings. A misclassified NIT with proven material effect is admitted by its actual consequence, not its label. Never dispatch both coverage slots in parallel on one candidate, and never more than one reviewer of a parent lineage in flight — checkpoint or closing. The second slot may be launched only after the first slot's terminal result is in and its admitted findings are repaired — repair landed suffices; the second slot's complete pass verifies it — or it returned no findings; until that gate is reached, the other slot is not dispatched. If the same defect nevertheless reaches main from both slots in one window, admit it once as cross-validated with family count one; it is not a repeated-family structural signal.

When B exposes a material defect, repair the authorized cone and revalidate A's invalidated conclusions (only while A is not retired as silent below). A repair of an admitted material finding is written against that finding's evidence class — a consumer-chain finding's fix cites the consumer anchors it now relies on — and is pre-checked in one single-point pass by the reviewer that produced the finding before the next formal delta or FINAL_BOUNDED round; an incomplete fix discovered one round later costs a whole wave. Revalidation may be bounded to the conclusions whose evidence base the repair actually changed; a ledger-recorded supersession replaces a fresh A dispatch only when the other slot's citation directly overturns A's named conclusion. Then B performs **FINAL_BOUNDED on the complete current PLAN or parent diff and its direct semantic neighbors**, including critical unchanged paths inside that boundary. Do not restrict final discovery to defects introduced by the last patch: a prior "covered" label is not proof of correctness. Retain unchanged source/test evidence and avoid restarting A's complete discovery or reviewing the entire repository. Reuse the same independent B reviewer where supported; otherwise use a fresh same-route continuation and record the actual identity. Acceptance still needs both slots current on the final candidate, original outcomes and required checks. No extra C reviewer or consecutive-success counter.

## Retiring a silent slot

A slot CLEAN in two consecutive rounds while the other slot produced material findings in that window is retired for the remainder of this candidate. A round is one dispatched review pass of that slot; only that slot's own consecutive CLEAN dispatches count, a material finding from it resets its streak, and the other slot's rounds do not interrupt it. Later repair deltas and the closing pass run on the productive slot alone, whichever label it holds: its complete FINAL_BOUNDED clean on the final boundary replaces the retired slot's invalidated-cone recheck, and the retired slot's earlier CLEAN stands as its coverage record. The trade is explicit — final-head independence is exchanged for cost once a slot is demonstrably silent. A slot whose post-repair revalidation cones yield no unique finding and no overturned conclusion across two consecutive material waves may likewise be retired, recorded with the wave references; its carried CLEAN then closes the pair as below. Retirement is per candidate (a new parent or PLAN restores both slots; corrections to the same PLAN are the same candidate; owner-directed scope changes are new candidates — see Budgets), stays inside the existing five-wave budget, and is recorded in the ledger with the two clean round references.

A NIT alone does not reset review. If main adopts a supposedly cosmetic suggestion that changes behavior, classify the actual delta and update affected evidence. Record original reviewer outcome, admission and actual closure separately. A failed required B followed only by A's assertion is still an open B; do not dispatch implementation. A budget stop or explicit user route change never becomes a fabricated CLEAN.

A compact final request names the candidate, original scope, closed material IDs and relevant evidence, without prescribing what B should conclude. Local repair checks can happen during the loop; the final acceptance still includes a genuinely complete bounded B pass on the final material candidate.

## Budgets stop loops, not correctness

All required PLAN reviews and delta closure **precede S01**. Use at most two material PLAN repair waves before stopping normal reviewer dispatch for diagnosis. A repair wave is one admitted repair cycle — findings assigned, repair landed, delta closure complete; findings repaired together in one cycle count as one wave, and a failed repair that must be redone starts a new wave. A repeated same-root-cause/no-progress attempt triggers diagnosis earlier. This is a provisional cost-control threshold, not a benchmark-derived optimum or permission to pass an unclean plan. Count globally across physical contexts/providers; renaming the task or final round does not reset it. An owner-directed scope change — outcomes added or removed by user authority, not by review findings — is a new candidate, not a rename: for the changed scope both slots and the repair budget restore, recorded in the ledger with the decision reference; the unchanged scope keeps its existing coverage.

Clustering is main's admission duty, not the reviewer's label: every admitted material finding is tagged with its root-cause family (for example "reads current deps instead of a captured context" or "no single owner for operation X"). A family that reaches two findings — within one report or across rounds — is a structural defect signal: the next step is a bounded structural decision (redesign the owner/invariant, re-section, or escalate to the advisor), not another local patch wave. Patch waves may continue only for findings outside every repeated family.

At diagnosis, distinguish wrong oracle, contradictory authority, newly introduced repair defect, routing error and genuinely unresolved design. Resolve a concrete user/owner decision, or escalate one bounded question to the external PLAN advisor ([advisor-escalation.md](advisor-escalation.md)); there is no model-level advisor route and no standing third reviewer. Resume only with a changed evidence-producing strategy and an explicit budget extension recorded by the decision owner. Otherwise leave PLAN_REVIEW_BLOCKED and do not start implementation. Code keeps the existing cumulative five-wave parent lineage and its hard-cap diagnosis; no fresh budget per child or provider.

## Identity, candidate and continuity

Record slot, actual role/transport/raw ID, requested and observed model/effort (UNKNOWN when absent), scope/base/head, original outcome, admission and invalidated coverage in existing state/report pointers. Wrong-route candidates may inform a decision but cannot fill an intended slot without an explicit authorized route change. Raw IDs stay within their own transport's record fields; never repurpose one transport's ID as another's. Do not pretend a route label means a real launch: only an actual dispatch with its terminal result counts as review evidence. No reviewer forwards its job through another agent as a proxy.

A reviewer session that ends between rounds may continue through its transport’s own continuity mechanism ([external-subagent.md](external-subagent.md) for plugin routes): the continuation retains the slot, finding and budget and is not a new independent Clean B. Every physical call stays recorded in the existing state/report pointers, distinct from logical rounds. Reconcile one transport failure before at most one bounded retry on the same route; missing runtime/model/effort or actual result is ORCHESTRATION_BLOCKED, not implicit fallback to main or another route.

Freeze the candidate while a reviewer is live: record the in-flight reviewer (slot, candidate head) in the section ledger and clear it on the terminal result; freeze and completion assertions check that record plus the host's actual running-task state, never memory alone. Children accumulate SUBSECTION_DELTA/PARENT_RECONCILIATION coverage within their parent slot; CHECKPOINT_VERIFIED is not parent acceptance. Serial successors await acceptance, checks and integration. Parallelism requires explicit isolation. Use actual terminal results; progress, early text and cancellation never open a barrier.
